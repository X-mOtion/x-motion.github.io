About

I am Beibei Jing. My research interests include computer vision, multimodal learning, and generative models for 3D human motion. I study how language and video can make motion synthesis more expressive, interpretable, and controllable.

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science and Technology from Huazhong University of Science and Technology in September 2026, advised by Prof. Junqing Yu. Previously, I received my M.Eng. from Yunnan University, advised by Prof. Hongwei Ding, and my B.Eng. from Henan Agricultural University.

My recent work explores video-augmented motion tokenization (MoVT), retrieval-augmented motion generation (RV2M), and interpretable anatomical motion diffusion (AMD).
