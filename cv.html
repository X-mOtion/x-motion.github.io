Research experience

Implicit human representations and text-driven interaction · NSFC Key Project

Responsible for cross-modal alignment and motion retrieval modules. Connected text and video features using X-CLIP and contrastive learning, modeled motion with transformers, and designed retrieval experiments for text-driven motion synthesis.

Realistic 2D digital humans · AIGC research and development

Contributed to motion editing and video generation. Built and annotated video training data, supported model training, and explored conditioning and style transfer with diffusion models.

Methods & research practice

Diffusion models, transformers, cross-attention, contrastive learning, motion tokenizers, and retrieval-augmented generation. Video dataset cleaning and annotation, model training, ablation studies, and evaluation using FID and R-Precision.
